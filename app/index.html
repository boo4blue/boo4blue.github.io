Use at your own risk; release of liability. By using Haven FW you accept that you use the listings, events, directions, guides and community chat at your own risk. To the fullest extent the law allows, you release the creators, volunteers and contributors of Haven FW from claims, losses or damages that come from using the app or from any third party it points to.

Community chat is public. Anything you post can be seen by others and is not confidential. Do not share your address, phone number, ID numbers or other private details. Be kind; messages that harass or endanger others may be removed.

Good to know
📞 Call ahead. Hours and availability change — a quick call saves a wasted trip.
🪪 Bring ID if you have it. Most places help without it, but ID speeds up intake.
🚌 Getting around. Citilink buses cover most of these stops — plan at citilink.com or call (260) 432-4546.
🍲 Tonight's meal. St. Mary's Soup Kitchen serves every day; the Rescue Mission serves 3 meals daily, 365 days a year.
👶 Can't care for a newborn? Indiana's Safe Haven law lets you surrender a baby up to 60 days old — anonymously, no arrest. Call 1-866-99BABY1 anytime, or read the Safe Haven guide.
About this app
About this app

Haven FW lists free shelters, food banks, hot meals, clinics, showers, thrift, bus passes, and benefit offices in Fort Wayne, Indiana — plus step-by-step guides for EBT, Medicaid, Medicare, WIC, disability, and free legal help, and a Jobs tab with temp agencies, day labor, and free job help, and a Chat tab to talk with others (pick a username tied to your phone, choose a profile icon, and hop between General, Ask for Help, and Free Stuff channels). Directory data was verified from public listings in September 2026 and is stored on your phone — the list works without internet. Map tiles need a connection. Made with 💜 for Fort Wayne.

Tool 03 · Handoff protocol

How they pass work

  1. Packet it

    Task, context, and a definition of done — never a vague “handle this.”

  2. Build it

    The receiving AI does the work on its own side, with its owner's tools.

  3. Cross-check it

    The sending AI reviews the result before either human ever sees it.

  4. Report it

    Results land back on the channel, in plain words, with sources attached.

The rulebook

Rules of engagement

R1 Owners approve

Money moves, purchases, account changes — the AIs prepare, the humans decide. Every time, no exceptions.

R2 Stay in your lane

Onion acts on Blaze's accounts; Lord acts on Michael's. Neither touches the other side's logins.

R3 Show your work

Claims come with sources, builds come with proof. “Trust me” is not a deliverable.

R4 Say it straight

If something can't or shouldn't be done, the AI says so on the channel — and offers what it can do instead.

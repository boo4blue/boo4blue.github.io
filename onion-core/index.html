Onion Core

Onion Core is Blaze's AI company, run day to day by Onion (Blaze's AI, acting as producer/CEO). Ten departments and 41 staff build Android apps, games, websites, music, and content — with Blaze holding the final word on everything.

What Onion Core makes

  • Onion AI — Blaze's personal AI app for Android, with chat, voice, and a built-in work surface.
  • Haven FW — a free, offline-first directory of food, shelter, showers, clinics, jobs, and help.
  • Onion everyday apps — Launcher, Browser, Notes, Messages, Phone, Calendar, Clock, Files, Gallery, Camera, Contacts, Weather, Calculator, and more.
  • Games — CultivationCraft RPG, Flappy Frenzy, Duskborn, Lumen Walk, Death Cart, the Scrapyard series, and more.
  • Music & content — original songs, manga, and video produced by the company's own teams.
